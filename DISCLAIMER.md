# Disclaimer

## What Determinist can't do
- Determinist is not a password manager
    - It doesn't store your passwords
    - It doesn't sync your passwords across devices
    - It doesn't know if the generated password and the one used on the site matches
- Determinist can't recover forgotten master passes

## Your responsibilities
- You have to remember your master passphrase
- You have to remember the inputs (presets coming soon!)
- You have to keep the master passphrase a secret
